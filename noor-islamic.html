Pilgrimage

Hajj & Umrah Guide

Step-by-step, with the authentic Dua or Dhikr for each stage. Where no specific Dua is established from a reliable source, that is stated plainly — a pilgrim may always recite general Dhikr, Dua or Quran instead.

Key: Authentic Sunnah a specific wording reported from the Prophet ﷺ · Qur'anic a verse recited at this stage · General/Permissible no single fixed wording is established — free Dhikr, Dua or Qur'an recitation is encouraged instead.
Charity

Zakat Calculator

Enter what you own below. Zakat (2.5%) is due once your total zakatable wealth reaches the Nisab threshold and a full lunar year has passed while holding it.

Nisab (the minimum threshold): traditionally set at 87.48 grams of gold or 612.36 grams of silver — whichever is lower is normally used, as it benefits more of the poor. Since metal prices change daily, enter today's price per gram below (check any gold/currency site) rather than relying on a fixed number here.

Today's metal prices (enter manually)

Your zakatable assets

This is a general calculation for common assets. Debts you owe are typically deducted first, and some categories (e.g. mixed business partnerships, agricultural produce, livestock) follow different rules. For your specific situation, please consult a qualified local scholar.

About Noor Islamic

A quiet corner of the internet for beneficial knowledge
